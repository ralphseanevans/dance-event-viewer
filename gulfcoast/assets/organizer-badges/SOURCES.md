# Organizer badge photo sources

## nina-travis-teacher-avatar.png (Nina & Travis, couple photo)

This badge is made from one real photo of Nina Outlaw and Travis Outlaw dancing West Coast Swing together. It contains no AI-generated, composited, or invented imagery.

**Photo used**
- https://dancingwithninatravisandandy.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/N-and-T-WCS.jpg (526x711 sepia JPEG, no C2PA or AI metadata).
  - Page: https://dancingwithninatravisandandy.com/about-us/ (the studio for the Foley, AL Tuesday series). The image appears directly under the "Travis Outlaw" heading, with alt text "Dance Instructors". The page says Travis is Nina's husband.
  - The same bytes are on Travis's author site at https://outlawbooksandbeyond.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/N-and-T-WCS.jpg, used on https://outlawbooksandbeyond.com/about-the-author/ with alt "Travis Outlaw".

**Edits**
- Background removed with rembg (u2netp, CPU).
- A polygon removed one piece of wall poster that rembg had left above Travis's head. That piece is background, not a person.
- Mild levels (8–236, gamma 0.92) and a 1.5 px mask smoothing.
- Placed on the same 1254 px transparent canvas as Todd & Jamie and Gary Jobst: head tops at y≈80, people clipped at the pill bottom (y1180), and a 28 px side fade.
- Same gold-rim Nunito 1000 name pill.
- Downscaled with LANCZOS to 336x336 RGBA.

The photo is sepia in the original and was not colorized. Nina's dark ponytail was lost against the dark background in the cut-out, and nothing was painted in to replace it.
